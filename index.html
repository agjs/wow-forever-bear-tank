            Consumables

              Professions, camping, Legacy points
              • While levelingLeatherworking and Skinning. Skin the animals you kill, then make your own leather armor, armor kits, and later the Wolfshead Helm and the Warbear pieces.
              • At 60Many tanks switch to Engineering plus Leatherworking. Engineering bombs (Sapper Charges) reportedly work in bear form in Forever.
              • AlwaysCooking, First Aid and Fishing. They don't use up a profession slot.
              • CampingForever lets you set up a camp with useful items. Wowhead recommends the Fish Bowl (more stats), First Aid Kit, Sharpening Wheel, Lodestone and Camp Tent (rested experience).
              • Legacy pointsForever's account-wide bonuses. Spend the first 5 on Thrill of Adventure, the next 5 on Well Rested, then Talented, then the rest on Thrill of Adventure.
